## 🎯 About Me

I'm a **19-year-old developer from the Netherlands** with a habit of asking how things work under the hood—and then trying to build them myself. What started as curiosity has turned into a genuine passion for clean code, new tools, and getting a little better every day.

When I'm not coding, you'll usually find me in **Los Santos**. I've been into GTA V since the PS3 era, and the engineering behind it—from the RAGE engine to the modding ecosystem—is what keeps me hooked.
